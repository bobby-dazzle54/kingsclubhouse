FAQ & find us

Questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

Hire starts from £250. Your quote depends on the space, the day and what you're planning. A bar minimum spend can replace the hire fee. Ask us for a quote.

Do you take a deposit?

Yes. The amount depends on the event and will be set out in your quote.

What if we need to cancel or change the date?

Our booking terms, including cancellations, come with your quote. Talk to us early and we'll do what we can.

How far ahead should we book?

Summer weekends and school holidays go first, so enquire as early as you can. For weddings, get in touch at least six months ahead.

Do you host weddings?

Yes, wedding celebrations and receptions, but only up to three a year. We're not licensed for ceremonies, so the legal ceremony takes place elsewhere, such as a register office or church, and the party comes back to us. The dates are hotly contested, so contact us at least six months in advance.

What time can we arrive to set up?

We'll agree set-up and clear-down times when you book.

What time do events finish?

All events finish by 11pm.

Can we bring our own food?

Yes. Bring your own caterer or cook in our outdoor kitchen. If you bring an external caterer, you're responsible for them, including their food hygiene rating and insurance.

Can we bring our own drinks?

The bar is run by the club, so drinks are bought at the bar. Ask us about minimum-spend packages and pre-ordered drinks for toasts.

Can we decorate?

Yes. Tell us your plans and we'll let you know what works in the space.

Can we have music?

Yes. We've hosted bands on an outdoor stage and DJs on the balcony.

Can we have a bouncy castle?

Yes. We work with a bouncy castle company we know and trust, and we can put you in touch. If you bring your own entertainers, you're responsible for them and their insurance.

Are children welcome?

Yes, we're a family venue. Children must be supervised by an adult from your party throughout.

Can we bring the dog?

Yes, dogs are very welcome, as long as they're well behaved and kept on a lead.

Can we use the pitches or put up a marquee?

From May to August, by arrangement. We look after our pitches carefully, so get in touch early.

Is the venue accessible?

Partly. The grounds, outdoor kitchen, marquee area and an accessible toilet are step-free. The bar and balcony are upstairs and there is no lift. Tell us your guests' needs and we'll plan the event around them.

Is there parking?

Yes, on site for around 100 cars. Motspur Park station is a 5-minute walk.

Will someone from the club be there?

Yes. Club staff are always on site during your event, and the bar is fully staffed.

Where does the money go?

Every booking helps fund the development of the club's grounds.
